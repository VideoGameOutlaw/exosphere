About

Every world here is one equation.

Exosphere draws each planet with a single fragment shader. Continents, oceans, cloud decks, ring shadows and the glow of the atmosphere are all computed per pixel, every frame, from a seed and a handful of numbers. Nothing is a texture or a model.

Pick a catalogued world, press N to discover a new one, tune it, then share the link. The same link always rebuilds the same planet.

  • Space pause
  • N new world
  • R reset view
  • H hide interface
  • 1–6 catalogue
  • ? this note
